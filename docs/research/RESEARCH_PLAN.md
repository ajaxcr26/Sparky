## Questions for each inspected example

1. What is the main rhythmic cell? Where are its rests, anticipations, pickups, and long notes?
2. Which pitches are repeated, approached stepwise, or reached by a leap? How does the contour return?
3. Which notes serve as anchors across chord changes? Which tensions resolve, and when?
4. What changes between adjacent bars, two-bar units, and the final turnaround?
5. How are lead, counterline, chord voicing, and bass separated in register and time?
6. Which perceived qualities come from MIDI writing, and which require timbre, articulation, effects or arrangement?
7. What is observable in this example, and what can reasonably be generalized beyond it?
